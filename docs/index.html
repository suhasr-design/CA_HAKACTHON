Fair Tickets

Anti-scalping ticketing where every ticket belongs to one verified human. Fans prove they're real with a zero-knowledge Aadhaar proof, join a lottery anyone can check, and can only resell at face value. No personal data is stored.

The problem

The fastest bot wins, and fans pay the markup

When a big match or concert goes on sale, bots and scripted accounts win the queue in minutes. Tickets come back on resale at several times face value. Today's fixes (captchas, KYC, OTPs) collect fans' personal data and still let scalpers through.

How it works

Six rules, enforced by smart contracts

1 · Verify once

One human, one entry

The fan scans their offline Aadhaar QR at a kiosk. A zero-knowledge proof, checked against real UIDAI keys, shows they're a unique person without revealing their name, number or photo.

2 · Fan Pass

Book in seconds

The one-time proof adds an anonymous pass to a group. Joining any event then takes a light phone proof (about 1–7 s). Each event sees a different code, so fans can't be tracked across events.

3 · Register

No 10 a.m. stampede

Registration stays open for a window. Gas is paid for fans, so nobody needs crypto. Fans without a smartphone use an ID desk with a capped quota.

4 · Draw

A lottery anyone can check

The organiser commits a secret before registration opens. It's mixed with a future block hash, and an on-chain shuffle orders every entrant. Anyone can recompute it. Seats 1–N win; everyone else is on a numbered waitlist.

5 · Resale

Face value forever

Tickets can't be transferred. A fan who can't attend sells back to the contract at face value, and the seat goes to the next person on the waitlist.

6 · Gate

Screenshots don't work

The fan's app scans a gate code that changes every 20 seconds and signs it. Copied QRs fail, and a ticket can't enter twice at different gates.

Results

Bot simulation: first-come queue vs Fair Tickets

76.9%of tickets taken by bots in a first-come queue

58 of 1,000 real fans got a ticket.

2.5%of tickets reached bots with Fair Tickets

488 of 1,000 real fans got a ticket, about 8× more.

This is a synthetic model, not a real sale: 500 seats, 1,000 fans, a scalper with 2,000 accounts plus 25 borrowed Aadhaars, 200 trials. Run it yourself with npm run simulate. Separately, 42 contract tests and end-to-end runs pass, and every attack we tested was rejected: forged proofs, double entry, fake or replayed gate QRs, ticket transfers and cross-gate reuse.

Transparency

What's real and what's a demo

Real

  • All contract logic, live on Quai Orchard testnet
  • Anon Aadhaar verification with real UIDAI public keys
  • Fan Pass, committed seat map, shuffle, buyback and waitlist
  • Rotating gate codes and cross-gate duplicate checks

Demo or not yet done

  • The live stage demo runs on a local copy of the chain so it can be reset
  • Synthetic crowd accounts, labelled on screen
  • The bot numbers come from a simulation
  • Not yet: security audit, mainnet, explorer source verification
